About Us

Mohindra Mechanical Works

Mohindra Mechanical Works welcomes you to a world of advanced printing and converting solutions built with innovation, precision and passion. With over 35 years of experience in the machinery manufacturing industry, we design and deliver a wide range of high-quality equipment, including Rotogravure Printing Machines, Lamination Machines, Coating Machines, Slitting Machines and Paper Bag Making Machines. All our machines are 100% made in India at our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, equipped with modern CNC tools, automated systems and skilled professionals who ensure accuracy in every process. Each machine is carefully tested for performance, efficiency and durability to meet the global standards of the printing and packaging industry. We take pride in exporting our machines to more than 25 countries across Asia, Africa, the Middle East and Europe. Our Machinery Categories

Explore Our Advanced Machinery Range

01Rotogravure Printing MachineOur Rotogravure Printing Machines are designed for printing on plastic films, paper, aluminium foil and flexible packaging materials. They deliver sharp print quality, consistent colour registration and smooth production for food packaging, pharmaceutical packaging and other industrial printing applications.Read More
02Lamination MachineOur Lamination Machines are used to bond films, paper, aluminium foil and other packaging materials into multilayer structures. Available in solventless, solvent-based, dry and wet lamination options, these machines support flexible packaging production with consistent bonding and smooth material handling.Read More
03Slitting and Rewinding MachineOur Slitting and Rewinding Machines are built to cut wide rolls of paper, plastic film, aluminium foil and laminated materials into smaller widths. They provide accurate slitting, controlled web tension and uniform rewinding for packaging and converting industries.Read More
04Coating MachineOur Coating Machines apply controlled layers of adhesive, lacquer, silicone and other coating materials onto paper, films and industrial substrates. Designed for consistent coating application and drying, these machines are suitable for packaging, adhesive tape, speciality paper and film processing.Read More
05Flexographic Printing MachineOur Flexographic Printing Machines are suitable for printing on paper, non-woven fabric, PP woven bags and flexible packaging materials. They support continuous roll-to-roll printing with reliable ink transfer and consistent print results for bags, wrappers and industrial packaging products.Read More
06Paper Bag Making MachineOur Paper Bag Making Machines convert kraft paper and other suitable paper rolls into bags for grocery stores, retail shops, food packaging and daily-use applications. These machines support regular bag production with controlled folding, cutting and pasting operations.Read More
07Metalized Transparent Film Window MachineOur Metalized Film Transparent Window Machines are designed to create clear window areas on metallized packaging films. They help manufacturers produce packaging with visible product sections while retaining the surrounding metallic finish, commonly used for food products and FMCG packaging.Read More
08Roll To Sheet Cutting MachineOur Roll To Sheet Cutting Machines convert continuous rolls of paper, film and other suitable materials into sheets of the required length. With controlled feeding and cutting operations, these machines are used for paper processing, packaging material preparation and industrial sheet production.Read More
09Embossing MachineOur Embossing Machines create raised, recessed and textured patterns on paper, aluminium foil, leather, rexine, non-woven fabric and selected films. These machines are used for decorative finishes, packaging materials and surface texturing where consistent pattern reproduction is required.Read More
10Rewinding MachineOur Rewinding Machines are used to transfer paper, aluminium foil, butter paper and flexible packaging films from one roll to another. They help maintain uniform roll formation, controlled tension and smooth material handling during inspection, roll preparation and converting operations.Read More
11Coming SoonPouch Making MachineOur upcoming Pouch Making Machines are planned for converting flexible packaging films into pouches for food, FMCG and other packaging applications. The range is expected to include different pouch formats, such as centre seal, side seal, three-side seal and gusset pouches. Read More
