Company Introduction

40 YEARS OF ENGINEERING EXCELLENCE

Over 2000 Machines Installed Worldwide. Mohindra Mechanical Works welcomes you to a world of advanced printing and converting solutions built with innovation, precision and passion. With over 35 years of experience in the machinery manufacturing industry, we design and deliver a wide range of high-quality equipment, including Rotogravure Printing Machines, Lamination Machines, Coating Machines, Slitting Machines and Paper Bag Making Machines. All our machines are 100% made in India at our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility, equipped with modern CNC tools, automated systems and skilled professionals. Each machine is carefully tested for performance, efficiency and durability to meet global printing and packaging standards. We export our machines to more than 25 countries across Asia, Africa, the Middle East and Europe.
At Mohindra Mechanical Works, we build long-term relationships through customized machinery solutions and complete support—from installation and training to after-sales service. Our reliable machines boost productivity, save energy and deliver superior output, ensuring smooth operations and minimal downtime.
