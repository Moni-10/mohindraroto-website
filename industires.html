Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about industrial printing, lamination, coating and converting machines.

Which industries use Mohindra Mechanical Works machines?+

Our machines are used in food and snack printing, pharmaceutical foil converting, paper and paper bag production, plastic films, labels, non-woven materials, industrial sacks and other roll-to-roll applications.

Do you manufacture printing, lamination and coating machines?+

We offer machine categories for rotogravure and flexographic printing, lamination, coating, embossing, slitting, rewinding and related converting processes. The suitable machine depends on your material and production requirements.

Can one machine handle every type of material?+

Not necessarily. Paper, BOPP, PET, aluminium foil and non-woven substrates can require different tension control, drying, coating and web-handling configurations. Share your substrate and specifications for a suitable recommendation.

Do you offer customized machinery?+

Machine selection and available configurations can be discussed based on working width, substrate, process, output and other production requirements.

How do I choose a slitting and rewinding machine?+

Start with your parent-roll width, material type, roll diameter, slit widths, desired winding quality and production speed. These details help determine a suitable configuration.

How can I send a machinery enquiry?+

Use the enquiry form below or call +91 9988440777. Include your material, required process, working width and approximate production target.
